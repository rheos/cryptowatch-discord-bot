# 📊 CryptoWatch Engagement System

Welcome to our new community engagement system! We're implementing this to build a more active, valuable community for everyone.

## 🎯 Why We're Doing This

- **Quality over Quantity**: We want engaged members, not lurkers
- **Reward Participation**: Active members get access to premium content
- **Build Community**: Everyone benefits from active discussion
- **Fight Information Asymmetry**: Share knowledge, gain knowledge

## 🏆 Membership Tiers

### 🆕 New Member
- **Access**: Welcome channels only
- **How to advance**: Post your introduction in #welcome-chat
- **Time limit**: None - take your time!

### 👤 Member  
- **Access**: All general channels
- **How to advance**: Be active for 30 days (10+ quality messages)
- **Benefits**: Full community access, all basic bot commands

### ⭐ Active Member
- **Access**: Premium channels + all member benefits
- **Requirements**: 10+ messages in the last 30 days
- **Benefits**:
  - 🎯 #premium-chat with experienced traders
  - 🚨 #advanced-alerts for extreme funding rates
  - 🔬 #research-collab for deep analysis
  - 🤖 Extended bot commands
  - 🎁 Early access to new features

## 📋 What Counts as Activity?

✅ **Good Activity**:
- Sharing market analysis
- Asking thoughtful questions
- Helping other members
- Posting trade ideas (win or lose!)
- Reacting helpfully (📈, 💡, ✅)

❌ **Doesn't Count**:
- Spam or low effort posts
- Just bot commands
- Only emoji reactions
- "GM" only (but GM is welcome!)

## 🏖️ Going on Vacation?

Active members can request @Vacation role to pause requirements for up to 30 days. Just ask an admin!

## ⏰ Timeline for Current Members

**Next 7 Days**: Grace period! Post a bit to ensure you qualify for Active status.

**After 7 Days**: 
- Members with 10+ messages in last 30 days → Automatic @Active role
- Everyone keeps current access
- Only NEW members start in welcome-only flow

**Every 30 Days**: Bot reviews activity and updates roles

## 🤖 Bot Commands

- `!mystats` - Check your engagement level
- `!help` - See all available commands
- `!negative` - Check funding rates (everyone can use)

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Will I lose access to channels I can see now?**
A: No! Current members keep access. This mainly affects new joins.

**Q: Is 10 messages a lot?**
A: That's less than 1 message every 3 days. Quality matters more than quantity!

**Q: What if I'm more of a reader?**
A: That's fine! You'll still have access to general channels. Premium channels are for contributors.

**Q: Can I lose Active status?**
A: Yes, if inactive for 30+ days. But you can earn it back anytime!

**Q: What about timezone differences?**
A: Activity is measured over 30 days, so timezone doesn't matter.
